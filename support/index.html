Support

Need a hand? Email support@duodoodles.fun and we'll get back to you.

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. Twodles is made for iPhone Duo, where folding the phone passes it along, but it plays on any iPhone running iOS 26 or later. On other iPhones, tap Done when you finish drawing, then pass the phone and tap to reveal.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the home screen, tap "Unlock all prompts", then "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can my family use my purchase?

Yes. The unlock works with Family Sharing.

Are my drawings saved or uploaded?

No. Drawings stay on your phone and disappear when you start a new round. Use the Share button on the reveal screen to save one to Photos or send it to a friend.

The fold isn't being detected.

Make sure you close the phone all the way. Your turn only ends once you've drawn something, so a blank canvas won't pass. If it still doesn't work, email us with your iOS version.
