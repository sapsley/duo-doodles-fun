Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Duo Doodles does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Drawings you make stay on your device. They're only shared or saved if you choose to, using the share button in the app.

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it doesn't send any data to us or to anyone else.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchases.

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
