Duo Doodles

Two artists are better than one.

How to play

  1. 1 Draw your secret prompt
  2. 2 Fold the phone and hand it to a friend
  3. 3 They draw their own secret prompt
  4. 4 Open it slowly for a drumroll reveal

Made for the fold

Each of you draws half a picture, like the top half of a monster and the bottom half of a beautiful lady, without ever seeing the other half. Folding the phone locks in your drawing, the closed phone turns into a composition notebook, and the drumroll speeds up as you open it for the reveal.

No iPhone Duo? It plays on any iPhone. Just pass the phone and tap to reveal.
